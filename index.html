Who We Are

Lived Experience & Cultural Safety

Peer2Peer Indigenous Society is rooted in community and guided by Métis and Indigenous peers who have walked the path. We bridge critical gaps in health and social systems by meeting our relatives right where they are—bringing dignity, cultural safety, and zero-judgment support directly to the streets of Victoria.

Specialties & Support

Our Mobile Outreach & Programs

Detox & Recovery Referrals

Guiding and connecting relatives to treatment and detox pathways.

Overdose Aftercare

Dedicated peer support team following critical overdose incidents.

Mobile Outreach Services

Boots-on-the-ground support traveling directly across Victoria.

Indigenous Harm Reduction

Culturally safe, peer-led care grounded in traditional values.

Wound Care & HIV Support

Essential health support, testing, and direct medical care access.

Relocation Outreach

Assistance with housing transitions and community navigation.
